# Chapter 2: The Rate of Profit

## Core Idea

This chapter introduces the rate of profit as the capitalist's practical measurement of success, distinct from the rate of surplus-value. Marx demonstrates that while surplus-value originates solely from variable capital's exploitation of living labour, the capitalist measures gain against the total capital advanced (p' = s/C = s/(c+v)). This transformation obscures the true source of surplus-value, creating the appearance that profit arises from capital as a whole rather than from labour exploitation. The chapter explains how this inversion of perspective is reinforced by the intertwining of production and circulation processes, market fluctuations, and the capitalist's practical consciousness.

---

## Frameworks Introduced

### I. Capital's Movement and the Profit Focus

#### The Capital Circuit
- **General Formula**: M-C-M' (sum of value thrown into circulation to extract larger sum)
- **Production Process**: Creates the larger sum (surplus-value)
- **Circulation Process**: Realises the larger sum
- **Capitalist Motive**: Not production for use-value or personal consumption

#### The Capitalist's True Interest
- **Product of Interest**: Not the palpable product itself, but the excess value over capital consumed
- **Total Capital Advance**: Capitalist advances total capital without regard to components' different roles in surplus-value production
- **Uniform Advance**: All components advanced uniformly to produce value in excess of capital
- **Exploitation Requirement**: Only way to convert variable capital value into greater value = exchange for living labour and exploit it
- **Simultaneous Advance**: Cannot exploit labour without advancing conditions for labour (means and subjects: machinery, raw materials)
- **Class Relation**: Capitalist owns means of production, confronts labourer as owner of only labour-power

#### The Capitalist's Indifference
- **No Distinction**: Doesn't matter whether constant capital advanced to profit from variable, or vice versa
- **Practical Reality**: Only variable capital creates surplus-value, but only if other portions (conditions of production) also advanced
- **Mutual Dependency**: Can exploit labour only by advancing constant capital; can realize constant capital only by advancing variable capital
- **Result**: Capitalist lumps all together in imagination

### II. From Rate of Surplus-Value to Rate of Profit

#### The Determining Measure
- **Actual Gain Rate**: Determined by proportion to total capital, not variable capital
- **Rate of Profit vs. Rate of Surplus-Value**: May remain same while expressing different surplus-value rates
- **Capitalist Perspective**: Only interested in surplus-value relation to total capital advanced

#### The Profit Definition
- **Surplus-Value Realization**: Excess value shaped in production, realized in circulation
- **Market Dependence**: Under competition, realization depends on market conditions
- **Alternative Realization**: Sale above or below value = different surplus-value division, not different surplus-value magnitude or nature

#### Circulation's Influence
- **Time Factor**: Circulation time exerts influence alongside working-time, limiting surplus-value realizable within given time
- **Intermingling**: Production and circulation processes intertwine, adulterating distinctive features
- **New Definitions**: Surplus-value and value production receive new definition in circulation
- **Metamorphoses**: Capital passes through circuit of metamorphoses
- **External Relations**: Capital enters relations with outer life (capital-capital, buyer-seller)
- **Conflated Determination**: Time of circulation and working-time cross paths; both seem to determine surplus-value

#### The Obscured Source
- **Original Form Disguise**: Capital and wage-labour confrontation disguised by intervening relations
- **Profit Appearance**: Surplus-value appears as excess of selling price over cost-price
- **Cost-Price Misrepresentation**: Easily represented as actual value (valeur intrinsèque)
- **Profit as Excess**: Appears as excess over immanent value

#### The Capitalist's Consciousness
- **Production Process**: Fleeting stage merging with circulation; surplus-value source inkling exists but as one factor among many
- **Circulation Misconception**: Surplus appears to originate in circulation independent of production
- **Historical Example**: Modern economists (Ramsay, Malthus, Senior, Torrens) identify circulation phenomena as proofs that capital in material existence is independent source of surplus-value alongside/without labour

#### The Wage Form Obscuration
- **Expenses View**: All parts (wages, raw materials, machinery depreciation) appear equally as expense sources
- **Surplus-Labour Specificity**: Extortion of unpaid labour loses specific character; appears as saving on expense article
- **Similar to Material Savings**: Like buying raw materials cheaper or machinery depreciation decreasing
- **Relation Obscured**: Specific relationship to surplus-value hidden

### III. The Rate of Profit Formula

#### The Two Measurements
- **Rate of Surplus-Value**: s/v (measured against variable capital)
- **Rate of Profit**: s/C = s/(c+v) (measured against total capital)
- **Different Proportions**: Two different measurements of same entity expressing different relations

#### The Transformation Priority
- **Deductive Order**: Must deduce surplus-value into profit transformation from rate of surplus-value into rate of profit, not vice versa
- **Historical Priority**: Rate of profit was historical point of departure
- **Visibility**: Surplus-value and rate of surplus-value = invisible essence requiring investigation
- **Surface Appearance**: Rate of profit and profit form = revealed on phenomenon surface

#### The Capitalist's Limited Perspective
- **Practical Interest**: Only interested in surplus-value relation to total capital advanced for commodity production
- **Inner Connection Obscured**: Specific relationship and inner connection of surplus with capital components fails to interest capitalist
- **Veiling Incentive**: Actually in capitalist's interest to draw veil over this relationship

#### The Inversion of Subject and Object
- **Production Inversion**: Subjective productive forces of labour appear as productive forces of capital
- **Capitalist Incarnation**: Value (past labour) dominating living labour incarnated in capitalist
- **Labourer's Position**: Appears as bare material labour-power, as commodity
- **Inverted Relationship**: Even in simple production relations produces inverted conceptions and transposed consciousness
- **Circulation Development**: Further developed by metamorphoses and modifications of actual circulation

### IV. The Independence of Profit Rate from Surplus-Value Rate

#### The Formal Difference
- **Rate of Profit**: s/C = surplus-value to total capital ratio
- **Measurement Difference**: Total capital includes consumed and merely employed portions
- **Expression**: Degree of self-expansion of total capital advanced
- **Inner Connection**: Ratio of variable capital amount of variation to advanced total capital magnitude

#### The Lack of Inner Relationship
- **Total Capital Value**: No direct inner relationship to surplus-value magnitude
- **Material Elements**: Constant capital (total minus variable) = material requisites for labour materialisation
- **Technical Relation**: Definite relation between labour quantity and means of production quantity
- **Surplus-Labour Relation**: Definite relation between surplus-value/surplus-labour and means of production

#### The Technical vs. Value Relation
- **Example**: Labour necessary for wage production = 6 hours daily
- **Surplus-Labour**: 12 hours work for 6 hours surplus-labour (100% surplus-value)
- **Means of Production Consumption**: Twice as much used in 12 hours as in 6 hours
- **No Direct Relation**: Surplus-value produced in 6 hours NOT directly related to means of production value used in 6 or 12 hours
- **Value Immateriality**: Means of production value immaterial; only technically required quantity matters
- **Price as Index**: Price may serve as index for required quantity as long as price doesn't change

#### The Mathematical Expression
- **Given Rate of Surplus-Value**: If s' known and magnitude given, rate of profit expresses different measurement of surplus-value
- **Phenomenal World**: In reality, surplus-value given as excess of selling price over cost-price; origin remains mystery
- **Magnitude Difference**: Profit magnitude may differ numerically from surplus-value

## Key Concepts

- **Rate of Profit (p')**: s/C or s/(c+v); surplus-value to total capital ratio
- **Rate of Surplus-Value (s')**: s/v; surplus-value to variable capital ratio
- **Cost-Price (k)**: Capitalist's cost; c + v; appears as actual value in capitalist perception
- **Profit**: Surplus-value appearing as excess of selling price over cost-price
- **Total Capital (C)**: c + v; sum of constant and variable capital
- **Capital Circuit**: M-C-M'; value thrown into circulation to extract larger value
- **Production-Circulation Intertwining**: Processes merge, obscuring surplus-value origin
- **Inversion of Subject and Object**: Labour's productive forces appear as capital's productive forces
- **Fetishized Capital**: Capital appears as independent source of surplus-value

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **The Capitalist's Practical Measure**: Rate of profit, not rate of surplus-value, is the capitalist's practical measure of success

2. **Total Capital Orientation**: Capitalist advances total capital and measures gain against it, not against variable capital alone

3. **The Necessary Unity**: Variable capital creates surplus-value only when constant capital also advanced; capitalist lumps all capital together

4. **Transformation Priority**: Rate of surplus-value to rate of profit transformation is deductive foundation; profit historically primary in capitalist consciousness

5. **Surface vs. Essence**: Rate of profit and profit appear on surface; surplus-value and its rate are invisible essence requiring investigation

6. **Production-Circulation Obscuration**: Intertwining of production and circulation processes obscures surplus-value's production origin

7. **Market Fluctuation Influence**: Circulation time and market conditions appear to determine surplus-value alongside working-time

8. **Wage Form's Obscuring Role**: Wages as price of labour (not labour-power) further hides exploitation relationship

9. **The Great Inversion**: Subjective productive forces of labour appear as capital's productive forces; capitalist as incarnation of dominating value

10. **No Inner Value Relation**: Total capital value has no direct inner relationship to surplus-value magnitude; only technical relations exist
