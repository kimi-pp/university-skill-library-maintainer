# Chapter 17: Changes of Magnitude in the Price of Labour-Power and in Surplus-Value

## Core Idea
The value of labour-power and surplus-value are determined by three factors: working day length, labour intensity, and labour productivity. Different combinations of these factors produce various effects on wages and surplus-value, revealing capital's strategies for exploitation.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Three Determinants of Labour-Power Value and Surplus-Value
- **Working Day Length (Extensive Magnitude)**: Total hours worked per day
- **Labour Intensity (Intensive Magnitude)**: Quantity of labour expended per time unit
- **Labour Productivity**: Quantity of product yielded per labour-time unit

### Three Fundamental Laws

**Law 1: Fixed Working Day**
- **Condition**: Working day length and labour intensity constant, productivity variable
- **Result**: Working day always creates same amount of value
- **Effect on value**: Individual commodity price varies, but total value created is constant
- **Example**: 12-hour day creates 6 shillings value regardless of productivity changes

**Law 2: Fixed Productivity**
- **Condition**: Productivity constant, working day and intensity variable
- **Result**: Value created varies directly with changes in working day length and intensity
- **Effect**: Longer/more intense day = more value; shorter/less intense = less value

**Law 3: Simultaneous Variation**
- **Condition**: All three factors variable simultaneously
- **Result**: Complex interactions depending on direction and magnitude of each change
- **Effect**: Must analyze each case individually based on specific combinations

### Four Main Combinations

**Combination 1: Productivity ↑, Intensity/Length Constant**
- **Labour-power value**: ↓ (cheaper necessaries)
- **Surplus-value**: ↑ (more produced in same time)
- **Wage**: May remain same or change depending on market

**Combination 2: Intensity ↑, Length/Productivity Constant**
- **Labour-power value**: → (no change in necessaries production)
- **Surplus-value**: ↑ (more labour expended in same time)
- **Effect**: More value created without changing working day length

**Combination 3: Length ↑, Intensity/Productivity Constant**
- **Labour-power value**: → (no change in necessaries)
- **Surplus-value**: ↑ (absolute surplus-value increases)
- **Effect**: Direct extension of surplus labour time

**Combination 4: Productivity ↑, Length ↑**
- **Labour-power value**: ↓ (cheaper necessaries)
- **Surplus-value**: ↑↑ (both absolute and relative surplus-value increase)
- **Effect**: Capital's ideal scenario for exploitation

## Key Concepts
- **Extensive Magnitude**: Working day length as factor in value creation
- **Intensive Magnitude**: Labour intensity as factor in value creation
- **Productivity**: Labour's effectiveness in producing use-values
- **Value of Labour-Power**: Determined by necessaries required by average labourer
- **Surplus-Value Magnitude**: Affected by all three factors in various combinations

## Key Takeaways
1. Three factors (length, intensity, productivity) determine labour-power value and surplus-value
2. Fixed working day always creates same total value, regardless of productivity
3. Capital seeks to vary all three factors to maximize surplus-value
4. Different combinations produce complex effects on wages and exploitation
5. Productivity increases can both reduce labour-power value and increase surplus-value
