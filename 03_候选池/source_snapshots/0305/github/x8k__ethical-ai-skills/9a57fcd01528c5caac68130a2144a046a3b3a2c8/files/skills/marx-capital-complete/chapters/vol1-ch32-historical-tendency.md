# Chapter 32: Historical Tendency of Capitalist Accumulation

## Core Idea
Primitive accumulation — the historical genesis of capital — resolves into the expropriation of the immediate producers, the dissolution of private property based on the labour of its owner. This process creates the capitalist mode of production, which in turn produces its own negation: the centralisation of capital and socialisation of labour that eventually burst the capitalist integument asunder, leading to the expropriation of the expropriators.

## Frameworks Introduced

### The Nature of Primitive Accumulation
- **Definition**: Not immediate transformation of slaves and serfs into wage labourers (mere change of form)
- **Essence**: Expropriation of immediate producers; dissolution of private property based on labour of its owner
- **Private property spectrum**: Exists where means and external conditions of labour belong to private individuals
- **Character difference**: Property has different character depending on whether private individuals are labourers or not
- **Petty industry**: Foundation is private property of labourer in means of production — essential for social production development and free individuality

### The Flourishing of Petty Production
- **Conditions**: Exists under slavery, serfdom, dependence but flourishes only where labourer is private owner of means of labour
- **Examples**: Peasant of cultivated land; artisan of handled tool as virtuoso
- **Presuppositions**: Parcelling of soil and scattering of other means of production
- **Exclusions**: Excludes concentration of means of production, cooperation, division of labour, social control of natural forces, free development of social productive powers
- **Compatibility**: Only compatible with narrow, primitive production systems and societies
- **Consequence**: Pecqueur — to perpetuate it would be "to decree universal mediocrity"

### The Dialectic of Dissolution
- **Internal contradiction**: At certain stage, petty production brings forth material agencies for its own dissolution
- **New forces**: New forces and passions spring up but old social organisation fetters them
- **Necessity**: Old organisation must be annihilated; it is annihilated
- **Process**: Transformation of individualised, scattered means into socially concentrated ones; pigmy property of many into huge property of few
- **Result**: Expropriation of mass from soil, means of subsistence, means of labour forms prelude to capital's history
- **Methods**: Series of forcible methods — epoch-making methods of primitive accumulation
- **Character**: Accomplished with merciless Vandalism under stimulus of most infamous, sordid, meanly odious passions

### The Two Forms of Private Property
- **Self-earned private property**: Based on fusing of isolated, independent labouring individual with conditions of labour
- **Capitalist private property**: Rests on exploitation of nominally free labour of others — wage labour
- **Transformation**: Self-earned property supplanted by capitalist property

### The New Phase: Capitalist Expropriation
- **Condition**: Process complete when old society decomposed; labourers turned into proletarians; means of labour into capital; capitalist mode stands on own feet
- **New expropriation**: No longer labourer working for himself but capitalist exploiting many labourers
- **Mechanism**: Accomplished by immanent laws of capitalist production itself — centralisation of capital
- **Dynamics**: One capitalist always kills many; hand in hand with centralisation develops cooperative form of labour process

### The Progressive Development Under Capitalism
- **Cooperative labour**: Develops on ever-extending scale
- **Scientific application**: Conscious technical application of science
- **Agricultural development**: Methodical cultivation of soil
- **Instrument transformation**: Instruments of labour become usable only in common
- **Economising**: All means of production economised by use as means of combined, socialised labour
- **World market**: Entanglement of all peoples in net of world market
- **International character**: Capitalist regime takes on international character

### The Contradiction Intensifies
- **Capital concentration**: Constantly diminishing number of magnates of capital usurp and monopolise all advantages
- **Growing misery**: Mass of misery, oppression, slavery, degradation, exploitation grows
- **Proletarian development**: Working class increases in numbers, becomes disciplined, united, organised by capitalist production mechanism
- **Fetter emergence**: Monopoly of capital becomes fetter upon mode of production
- **Bursting point**: Centralisation of means of production and socialisation of labour become incompatible with capitalist integument
- **Result**: Integument burst asunder; knell of capitalist private property sounds

### The Dialectical Negation
- **First negation**: Capitalist mode of appropriation (result of capitalist production) produces capitalist private property — first negation of individual private property based on labour of proprietor
- **Second negation**: Capitalist production begets its own negation with inexorability of law of Nature
- **Negation of negation**: Does not re-establish private property for producer but gives individual property based on acquisitions of capitalist era
- **New basis**: Co-operation and possession in common of land and means of production
- **Historical law**: Transformation of scattered private property into capitalist private property is more protracted, violent, difficult than transformation of capitalist property into socialised property

### The Historical Comparison
- **First expropriation**: Expropriation of mass of people by few usurpers
- **Second expropriation**: Expropriation of few usurpers by mass of people
- **Historical progression**: Second transformation easier because already rests on socialised production

### Sismondi's Insight
- **New condition**: "Nous sommes dans une condition tout-à-fait nouvelle de la societé... nous tendons à séparer toute espèce de propriété d'avec toute espèce de travail" (We are in an entirely new situation... we strive to separate every kind of property from every kind of labour)

### The Communist Manifesto's Contribution
- **Industry's role**: Advance of industry (involuntary promoter: bourgeoisie) replaces isolation of labourers (due to competition) by revolutionary combination (due to association)
- **Self-destruction**: Modern Industry cuts from under bourgeoisie's feet the foundation on which it produces and appropriates
- **Gravediggers**: Bourgeoisie produces above all its own grave-diggers; its fall and proletariat's victory equally inevitable
- **Revolutionary class**: Of all classes facing bourgeoisie, only proletariat is truly revolutionary
- **Other classes**: Lower middle classes, small manufacturers, shopkeepers, artisans, peasants fight bourgeoisie to save their middle-class existence
- **Reactionary character**: They are reactionary because they try to roll back the wheel of history

## Key Concepts
- **Primitive Accumulation**: Historical genesis of capital through expropriation
- **Private Property**: Means and conditions of labour belonging to private individuals
- **Petty Industry**: Small-scale production based on labourer's ownership of means
- **Capitalist Private Property**: Property based on exploitation of wage labour
- **Centralisation of Capital**: Concentration of capital through competition and expropriation
- **Socialisation of Labour**: Development of cooperative, social forms of production
- **Negation of Negation**: Dialectical process of capitalist development and overcoming
- **Expropriators Expropriated**: Final phase where capitalists are expropriated by proletariat
- **Capitalist Integument**: The capitalist form that becomes incompatible with socialised production
- **World Market**: Global network of capitalist relations

## Key Takeaways
1. Primitive accumulation is the expropriation of immediate producers and dissolution of labour-based private property
2. Petty production flourishes when labourers own their means of production but is incompatible with socialised production
3. Capitalism arises through violent expropriation but contains within it the seeds of its own destruction
4. Capitalist development produces centralisation of capital and socialisation of labour on an ever-extending scale
5. As capital centralises, misery and exploitation increase, but so does proletarian organisation and consciousness
6. The monopoly of capital eventually becomes a fetter on production itself
7. Capitalist private property is the negation of individual labour-based property; socialist property is the negation of this negation
8. The transformation from capitalist to socialised property is historically easier because capitalism already rests on socialised production
9. The expropriation of the expropriators is the inevitable conclusion of capitalist development
10. The bourgeoisie produces its own grave-diggers — the proletariat — whose victory is as inevitable as bourgeoisie's fall
