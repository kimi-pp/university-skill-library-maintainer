# Chapter 20: Time-Wages

## Core Idea
Time-wages (day-wages, week-wages) are the converted form of labour-power's value, where payment is made for a definite period of work. The nominal wage (money received) may remain constant while the price of labour (value per hour) falls if the working day is extended.

## Frameworks Introduced

### Time-Wages Definition
- **Form**: Labour-power sold for a definite period (day, week, etc.)
- **Appearance**: Payment for time worked
- **Reality**: Price of labour-power expressed as payment per time period
- **Basis**: "The sale of labour-power takes place for a definite period of time"

### Nominal vs. Real Wages
- **Nominal Wages**: The sum of money received for daily/weekly labour
- **Real Wages**: The quantity of use-values (commodities) that money can buy
- **Relation**: Nominal wages may stay same while real wages fall (or vice versa) due to price changes
- **Distinction**: Corresponds to exchange-value vs. use-value of labour-power

### Price of Labour Calculation
- **Formula**: Price of labour = Daily value of labour-power / Number of hours in working day
- **Example**: Daily labour-power value = 3s, working day = 12h → Price per hour = 3d
- **Function**: Serves as unit measure for the price of labour
- **Implication**: Same daily wage can represent different prices of labour depending on day length

### Constant Nominal Wages, Falling Price of Labour
- **Mechanism**: Working day extension without wage increase
- **Example**: Daily wage = 3s, working day 10h → 3.6d/hour; working day 12h → 3d/hour
- **Result**: 20% reduction in price of labour with same nominal wage
- **Capitalist advantage**: More surplus-value extracted without raising wages

### Time-Wages and Surplus-Value
- **Relation**: Laws of surplus-value (Ch17) apply to time-wages through simple transformation
- **Formula**: Surplus-value = (Daily wage × Hours of surplus labour) / Hours of necessary labour
- **Effect**: Time-wages make the calculation of surplus-value more transparent

### Quality of Labour Considered
- **Average labour**: Wages based on average social labour quality
- **Above/below average**: Workers of different efficiency receive different effective wages
- **Payment form**: Usually paid by time, but quality affects actual value received

## Key Concepts
- **Time-Wages**: Payment for labour-power per time period
- **Nominal Wages**: Money amount received
- **Real Wages**: Purchasing power of nominal wages
- **Price of Labour**: Value per hour of labour (nominal wage / hours worked)
- **Daily/Weekly Wages**: Specific forms of time-wages
- **Unit Measure**: Price per hour as standard for comparing labour prices

## Key Takeaways
1. Time-wages are the price of labour-power expressed per time period
2. Nominal wages can remain constant while the price of labour falls (through working day extension)
3. Price of labour = daily labour-power value / working day hours
4. Time-wages transform the laws of surplus-value into wage laws
5. Nominal vs. real wage distinction is crucial for understanding worker living standards
