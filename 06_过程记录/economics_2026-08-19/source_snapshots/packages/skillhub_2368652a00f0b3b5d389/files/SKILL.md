## Chapter 1 — FAQ (0-basic) / 第 1 章 — 0 基础常见问答

**Q:** I've never used any membership or check-in system — where do I even start?
**A:** Start with the "Prehistoric Escape Route" (`playbooks/01` Ch.0): keep a disciplined Excel ledger (one row per member, phone as key) until you exceed ~300 active members, use prepaid balances, or have ≥2 staff editing the same file — then buy your first SaaS. Your first SaaS should be a Membership Management System (MMS); confirm a data-export clause before signing (Iron Law 8).

**问：** 我从来没用过会籍或签到系统，到底从哪开始？
**答：** 走「脱贫四步路线」（`playbooks/01` 第0章）：先保持有纪律的 Excel 台账（每人一行、手机号为主键），直到出现「活跃会员 >300 人、已用预付余额、或 ≥2 名员工同编一表」任一情况，再买第一套 SaaS。第一套应选会籍管理系统（MMS）；签约前务必确认数据导出条款（铁律 8）。

---

**Q:** Which software should I buy first?
**A:** Buy the MMS first (`references/06` #1). It is the system of record for members, contracts, check-ins and billing, and everything else (booking, POS, access, BI) plugs into it. Weight "data export" and "offline check-in" highest, and score ≥3 vendors including one local-market option.

**问：** 我应该先买哪套软件？
**答：** 先买会籍管理系统 MMS（`references/06` 第1类）。它是会员、合同、入场、收费的记录中枢，其余系统（约课、收银、门禁、BI）都接入它。选型时把「数据导出」和「离线签到」权重放最高，并对比 ≥3 家供应商（含本地选项）。

---

**Q:** The gate won't open during peak hours and there's a queue at the door — what now?
**A:** Follow runbook R1 (`playbooks/08` #r1): confirm it is WAN/local gear, switch the gate to fail-open or a manual roster, post a "check in at desk" sign, and log entries on paper (name + time + package). Never prop the gate open without a manual log — you lose track of who is inside (`data/21` #gate-propped-no-log). Reconcile the paper roster against the system before close.

**问：** 高峰时段闸机不开、门口排长队，怎么办？
**答：** 按 Runbook R1（`playbooks/08` #r1）：先确认是网络还是本地设备故障，将闸机切到故障开或人工名单，贴「请到前台核验入场」告示，纸质登记入场（姓名+时间+套餐）。绝不可只撑开闸机而不记账——会不知谁在场（`data/21` #gate-propped-no-log）。闭店前把纸质名单与系统对账。

---

**Q:** We want face-entry. What are the compliance rules?
**A:** Biometric data is "sensitive personal information" in every APAC market. You must (1) offer a non-biometric alternative (QR / RFID / PIN) — never face-only; (2) obtain separate, explicit consent; (3) post camera/CCTV signage; (4) strictly ban any imaging device in changing rooms/showers (HI-5). Verify the exact article + retrieval date via `tools/05` (`references/10`, `references/12`).

**问：** 我们想做人脸入场，合规上要注意什么？
**答：** 在亚太每个市场，生物识别都属「敏感个人信息」。必须：(1) 提供非生物识别替代方式（二维码/卡/密码），绝不可仅人脸；(2) 取得单独、明确的同意；(3) 张贴摄像头/CCTV 标识；(4) 更衣室/淋浴间绝对禁止任何成像设备（HI-5）。具体条款与检索日经 `tools/05` 核验（`references/10`、`references/12`）。

**Q:** How do we actually start using AI without a data team?
**A:** Follow the zero-basis rule (`references/04`): never prescribe an L4 solution to an L1 club. Start at FDMM L1 with Tier-3 AIGC content (posters, short-video scripts, bilingual copy, AIGC labeling) using free/low-cost LLM tools — no data team needed. At L2, add an AI customer-service bot (ai-01). Churn/CV/LLM-CS features come only at L3+ with measured ROI.

**问：** 没有数据团队，到底怎么开始用 AI？
**答：** 遵守 0 基础规则（`references/04`）：绝不给 L1 场馆上 L4 方案。从 FDMM L1 起步，用第三档 AIGC 内容（海报、短视频脚本、双语文案、AIGC 标识）配合免费/低成本 LLM 工具——无需数据团队。到 L2 再加 AI 客服机器人（ai-01）。流失预测/CV/大模型客服等要到 L3+ 且 ROI 可测才上。

**Q:** We want to expand to multiple APAC markets. What's the path?
**A:** Expansion is gated by per-market compliance (`references/10`/`references/11`, `playbooks/05`). For each new market, map the "four-pack": ① privacy/data law, ② biometric & CCTV rules, ③ payments & prepaid (e.g. China 单用途预付卡 filing, SG CaseTrust), ④ industry-specific (cooling-off, safety, minors). Run `tools/05` for exact articles + cross-border data rules (HI-9) before launch. Do not copy-paste one market's setup to another (Iron Law 1).

**问：** 我们想扩张到多个亚太市场，路径是什么？
**答：** 扩张受每市场合规约束（`references/10`/`references/11`、`playbooks/05`）。每个新市场都要做「四件套」映射：① 隐私/数据法，② 生物识别与监控规则，③ 支付与预付（如中国单用途预付卡备案、新加坡 CaseTrust），④ 行业特有（冷静期、安全、未成年人）。上线前跑 `tools/05` 查具体条款与跨境数据规则（HI-9）。切勿把一个市场的方案照搬另一市场（铁律 1）。

**Q:** How do I avoid vendor lock-in?
**A:** Iron Law 8 + `references/05` §11: always offer ≥3 options (incl. one local + one open/low-cost), score on lock-in risk and data-export, and keep a tested data-export path (your BATNA). Review the data-export clause BEFORE signing; delete auto-renewal traps and uncapped price-hike clauses. Keep a second vendor option ready at every renewal.

**问：** 怎么避免被供应商锁死？
**答：** 铁律 8 + `references/05` 第11节：任何推荐至少 3 个选项（含本地 + 开源/低成本），按锁定风险与数据导出打分，并保留已实测的数据导出通道（即你的 BATNA）。签约前审数据导出条款；删掉自动续约陷阱与无上限涨价条款。每次续约都备好第二供应商。

**Q:** What are the prepaid / stored-value regulations I must know?
**A:** Prepaid balances are a liability, not revenue (HI-3). In China, stored-value membership is regulated as 单用途预付卡 — issuers above thresholds must file with commerce authorities and use a bank-based fund custody/escrow account, separating prepaid funds from operating cash. In Singapore, CaseTrust accreditation is often expected by malls. Always give a written contract + refund rules. Verify thresholds via `tools/05`.

**问：** 预付/储值相关的法规我必须知道哪些？
**答：** 预付余额是负债而非收入（HI-3）。在中国，储值会籍按「单用途预付卡」监管——超阈值发卡须向商务主管部门备案并使用银行资金存管账户，预付资金与运营现金隔离。新加坡常需 CaseTrust 认证（商场多采信）。务必出具书面合同与退费规则。阈值经 `tools/05` 核验。

**Q:** We run a 24h unmanned club. How do we keep it safe?
**A:** 24h unmanned is L3+ by design and must keep human oversight redundancy (HI-2): access gate + surveillance + a remote monitoring center + fail-safe egress. CV safety monitoring (ai-38) detects falls in unmanned hours but must keep a human in the loop (remote center + escalation) — never fully autonomous. Lone-exerciser protection and a remote alarm are mandatory (`references/02` 24h format).

**问：** 我们开 24 小时无人店，怎么保证安全？
**答：** 24h 无人业态设计上即 L3+，必须保留人工监管冗余（HI-2）：闸机 + 监控 + 远程监控中心 + 故障安全疏散。CV 安全监控（ai-38）可在无人时段检测跌倒，但必须保留人在回路（远程中心 + 升级机制）——绝不可全自动。独自锻炼者保护与远程报警是强制项（`references/02` 24h 业态）。

**Q:** Can I use churn prediction? Is it reliable?
**A:** Churn prediction (ai-04) needs FDMM L3+ and ≥6 months of attendance/login/payment behavior. Start as a vendor add-on on your MMS, run it as a high-ROI pilot, and require a bias audit so the model does not discriminate by protected attributes (HI-7 save-outreach needs consent). Do NOT trust it at L1–L2.

**问：** 我能不能用流失预测？可靠吗？
**答：** 流失预测（ai-04）需 FDMM L3+ 及 ≥6 个月的出勤/登录/缴费行为。先用会籍 SaaS 的供应商插件起步，作为高 ROI 试点运行，并做偏见审计，确保模型不按受保护属性歧视（挽留触达须同意，HI-7）。L1–L2 阶段不要轻信它。

**Q:** How do I prove ROI before spending money?
**A:** Iron Law 6 + `tools/06`: any recommendation above ¥100k (or market equivalent) must carry a three-scenario ROI — base / expected / pessimistic — separating revenue, cost, compliance and member-experience benefits. The pessimistic scenario must include "no adoption" and "vendor fails" tails. Ranges, not single points (Iron Law 11).

**问：** 花钱前怎么证明 ROI？
**答：** 铁律 6 + `tools/06`：任何超过 10 万元（或等值）的投资都要附 ROI 三情景——基准/预期/悲观——区分营收、成本、合规与会员体验四类收益。悲观情景必须含「没人用」与「供应商破产」尾部。给区间而非单点（铁律 11）。

**Q:** We had a data breach. What do we do in the first 72 hours?
**A:** Run runbook R4 (`playbooks/08` #r4): 0–4h contain & assess (disconnect the system, preserve logs, do NOT wipe); 4–24h run `tools/05` for the market deadline and notify the regulator where required; 24–72h notify affected members + force credential reset; 72h+ patch, rotate all keys, postmortem. Per-market windows are directional — verify via `tools/05` (e.g. CN→CAC, SG→PDPC, KR→PIPC 24–72h).

**问：** 我们发生了数据泄露，头 72 小时怎么办？
**答：** 跑 Runbook R4（`playbooks/08` #r4）：0–4 小时遏制与评估（断开系统、保留日志、切勿擦除）；4–24 小时跑 `tools/05` 查市场时限，必要时上报监管；24–72 小时通知受影响会员并强制重置凭据；72 小时后修补、轮换所有密钥、复盘。各市场时限为方向性——经 `tools/05` 核验（如中国→网信办、新加坡→PDPC、韩国→PIPC 24–72 小时）。

**Q:** How do I choose which market to enter first?
**A:** Score candidate markets on the same four-pack used for compliance: privacy/data maturity, biometric & CCTV strictness, prepaid/fund-supervision burden, and industry-specific friction (cooling-off, safety, minors). Lower-friction, better-understood markets are easier first wins; map each with `tools/05` and match to your FDMM level (Iron Law 7). Don't enter a market whose compliance you haven't verified.

**问：** 我该怎么选先进哪个市场？
**答：** 用与合规相同的「四件套」给候选市场打分：隐私/数据成熟度、生物识别与监控严格度、预付/资金监管负担、行业特有摩擦（冷静期、安全、未成年人）。摩擦低、更熟悉的市场更易作为首胜；用 `tools/05` 逐一映射，并匹配自身 FDMM 等级（铁律 7）。没核验合规的市场不要进。

**Q:** When should I hire a dedicated IT person?

Hire dedicated IT when you are multi-club, or exceed ~500 members with multiple integrated systems, or when incident/SLA load outgrows one person. As a rule, ticketing/helpdesk (references/06 #15) becomes needed at L2–L3 when >1 club or >500 members.

**问：** 我什么时候该雇专职 IT 人员？

  - Buy unless the function is your core differentiator AND you have the people
  - L1–L3: never build a membership CRM from scratch — buy, then integrate

## Chapter 3 — Ten Commandments / 第 3 章 — 健身数字化十大戒律

1. **Format-fit first.** Name the club format and market before any advice — no universal solution. / **业态适配优先。** 任何建议先点明业态与市场，没有通用方案。
2. **Life-safety is non-negotiable.** AI may assist pools, AED, 24h and fire-egress — it may never replace mandated human supervision. / **人身安全不可谈。** AI 可辅助泳池、AED、无人时段与消防疏散，绝不可替代法定人工监督。
3. **Compliance built-in.** Map privacy + biometric + payment + industry rules with law, version, article and retrieval date. / **合规内置。** 以「法+版本+条款+检索日」映射隐私、生物识别、支付与行业规则。
4. **Gate every deliverable.** Pass the rigor gates (G1–G13) before any release; gate fails → no release. / **交付必过闸。** 产出前过严谨性闸门（G1–G13），不过不出稿。
5. **Follow the lifecycle.** Diagnose → Strategy → Selection → Investment → Implementation → Go-live → Operations → Retrospective. Skipping stages kills projects. / **流程须遵循。** 诊断→战略→选型→投资→实施→上线→运营→复盘，跳阶段是失败头号原因。
6. **Prove ROI first.** Above ¥100k, show base/expected/pessimistic scenarios across revenue, cost, compliance and experience. / **先证 ROI。** 超 10 万元须给基准/预期/悲观三情景，覆盖营收、成本、合规与体验。
7. **Evolve, don't leap.** Progress L1→L5 with quantified entry criteria; never jump paper club to AI-native unmanned. / **演进不跳级。** 按 FDMM L1→L5 递进、每级有准入，纸表馆勿直跳 AI 无人。
8. **Stay vendor-neutral.** Offer ≥3 options (local + open/low-cost), flag lock-in, review data-export before signing. / **供应商中立。** 至少 3 选项（本地+开源），标锁定风险，签约前审数据导出。
9. **Data is sovereign and minimal.** State where data lives, whether it crosses borders, collect the least; biometric local-first where required. / **数据主权且最小化。** 说清存放地、是否出境，最小化采集，生物识别按市场本地优先。
10. **Honesty over completeness.** Pilot first if immature, say "not worth it" if ROI fails, retrieve if unsure — never fabricate. / **诚实优于完美。** 不成熟先试点，ROI 不过说"不值"，存疑就检索，绝不编造。

## Chapter 5 — Crisis & BCP Runbooks Summary / 第 5 章 — 危机与业务连续性 Runbook 摘要

**1. Network Down, Club Open (R1) / 断网但营业（R1）**
- EN: 0–5 min confirm WAN, try 4G/5G failover; 5–10 min switch POS offline + gate fail-open/manual roster + "pay at desk" sign; 10–15 min log paper roster; 15–30 min call ISP for ticket #+ETA; on restore re-sync & reconcile; before close lock the day.
- 中文：0–5 分确认广域网、试备份 4G/5G；5–10 分 POS 切离线、闸机故障开/人工名单、贴「前台付款」；10–15 分纸质名单登记；15–30 分致电 ISP 拿工单号+ETA；恢复后重新同步对账；闭店前锁定当日。

**2. Power Outage (R2) / 停电（R2）**
- EN: 0–2 min confirm gate fail-OPEN for egress (HI-4), usher members calmly; 2–5 min check UPS window, graceful POS close if >50% battery; 5–10 min shutdown order: member devices → POS → gate → switches → server last; on restore power up reverse, test gate+POS. Injury → call ambulance + AED path first.
- 中文：0–2 分确认闸机断电开（HI-4）、平稳引导会员；2–5 分查 UPS 窗口，电量>50% 让 POS 体面收尾；5–10 分关机顺序：会员设备→POS→闸机→交换→服务器最后；来电逆序开机、测闸机+POS。有人受伤先呼救+AED 路径。

**3. Payment System Down (R3) / 收银支付故障（R3）**
- EN: 0–5 min confirm both rails down (¥1 test each); 5–15 min switch to alt collection (backup wallet QR or paper voucher), log every amount+member; 15–30 min call gateway vendor; on restore settle vouchers vs system; before close zero gap, lock day.
- 中文：0–5 分确认两通道皆瘫（各试 ¥1）；5–15 分切替代收款（备用钱包码或纸质代金券），逐笔记金额+会员；15–30 分致电支付网关；恢复后代金券与系统结算；闭店前零差异、锁日。

**4. Data Breach — 72h (R4) / 数据泄露 72 小时（R4）**
- EN: 0–4 h contain & assess, preserve logs, do NOT wipe; 4–24 h run `tools/05` for deadline + notify authority; 24–72 h notify members + force credential reset; 72 h+ patch, rotate keys, postmortem. Verify per-market window (CN→CAC, SG→PDPC, KR→PIPC 24–72h).
- 中文：0–4 小时遏制评估、留日志、勿擦；4–24 小时跑 `tools/05` 查时限并上报监管；24–72 小时通知会员并强制重置凭据；72 小时后修补、轮换密钥、复盘。各市场时限经 `tools/05` 核验（中国→网信办、新加坡→PDPC、韩国→PIPC 24–72h）。

**5. Injury / AED Emergency / 受伤与 AED 急救**
- EN: From the Universal First-15-Minutes script (A10): 0–1 min protect people — anyone injured/danger → call emergency + clear AED path ABOVE any IT fix (HI-2); then contain, log, match runbook, comms. In R2, injured member → ambulance + `references/16` §S.
- 中文：源自通用前 15 分钟脚本（A10）：0–1 分保人——有人受伤或危险先呼急救 + 清出 AED 路径，高于任何 IT 修复（HI-2）；继而控制、记录、对卡、沟通。R2 中受伤会员→呼救 + `references/16` §S。

**6. Biometric / Face-Entry Failure / 人脸/生物识别入场失效**
- EN: Treat as gate fail-open (R1 step 2): switch to non-biometric alternative (QR/RFID/PIN — always offered per HI-5/consent), manual roster, post sign. Never force the arm; keep fail-safe egress. If a reader fails, use a known-valid band and log (`data/10` #c2-gate). Never guess on biometrics/fire.
- 中文：按闸机故障开处理（R1 第 2 步）：切非生物识别替代方式（二维码/卡/密码——按 HI-5/同意须常备）、人工名单、贴告示。勿硬掰臂；保留故障安全疏散。读头故障用已知有效手环并记账（`data/10` #c2-gate）。生物识别/消防绝不猜。

## Chapter 6 — Vendor Selection Quick Guide / 第 6 章 — 供应商选型速查

**The ≥3-options rule / ≥3 选项规则**
- EN: Iron Law 8 + `references/05` §11 + `data/03`: for every category, compare ≥3 vendors including at least one local-market option and, where viable, one open-source/low-cost option. Score on lock-in risk and data-export capability.
- 中文：铁律 8 + `references/05` 第11节 + `data/03`：每类至少比 3 家，含本地选项与可行开源/低成本选项，按锁定风险与数据导出能力打分。

**Key evaluation dimensions / 关键评估维度**
- EN: (1) Data-export (CSV/API, on demand, no lock-in); (2) local payment & prepaid compliance; (3) native connectors (booking/access/app/BI); (4) multi-club & multi-role permissions; (5) local-language UI; (6) lock-in risk; (7) pricing transparency (one-time vs recurring separated); (8) SLA (uptime, response, penalty); (9) warranty/spare-parts availability ≥ product life (`references/07`).
- 中文：(1) 数据导出（CSV/API、随时、无锁定）；(2) 本地支付与预付合规；(3) 原生对接（约课/门禁/App/BI）；(4) 多店多角色权限；(5) 本地语言后台；(6) 锁定风险；(7) 价格透明（一次性与 recurring 分开）；(8) SLA（在线率、响应、罚则）；(9) 保修与备件可得 ≥ 产品寿命（`references/07`）。

**Data-export clause warning / 数据导出条款警告**
- EN: Golden rule (`references/06`, `data/03`, `data/21#ap-021`): no written data-export clause → no signature. Require full member + contract + transaction export in open format, ≤30 days, fixed/zero fee, signed BEFORE ink (Iron Law 8). Refusing data export is a red line — fire the vendor.
- 中文：黄金铁律（`references/06`、`data/03`、`data/21#ap-021`）：无书面数据导出条款 → 不签字。要求整套会员/合同/交易以开放格式导出、≤30 天、固定/零费用，签约前落字（铁律 8）。拒绝数据导出即红线——换掉该供应商。

**Red flags / 红旗**
- EN: (1) No data-export clause or "export costs extra / takes months"; (2) auto-renewal trap + uncapped price-hike clauses; (3) single shared admin login for all staff (`data/21#ap-009`); (4) "you're too small for a written contract"; (5) vendor refuses to answer the 6 security questions (`references/16` §J: where stored, export, MFA, audit log, restore test, breach notice); (6) repeated SLA breaches with no remediation; (7) price hike beyond market with no added value; (8) compliance gap threatening HI-1~HI-9.
- 中文：(1) 无数据导出条款或「导出另收费/要好几个月」；(2) 自动续约陷阱 + 无上限涨价条款；(3) 全员共用一个管理员账号（`data/21#ap-009`）；(4)「你太小不用书面合同」；(5) 拒绝回答 6 个安全问题（`references/16` §J：存放地、导出、MFA、审计日志、恢复演练、泄露通知）；(6) 反复违反 SLA 且不整改；(7) 涨价超市场且无增值；(8) 触及 HI-1~HI-9 的合规缺口。

## 1 · ROI Three-Scenario Worked Example / ROI 三情景算例

**EN — Churn-AI buy (¥100k-equiv), single mid-market club, 5,000 members, 12% annual churn = 600 lost/yr, avg member LTV ¥3,000.**

- Revenue: 4pp churn drop → 200 members retained × ¥3,000 = **+¥600k/yr**
- Cost: 2 coach-hours/week saved on manual churn calls = **+¥30k/yr**
- Compliance: 0 (none claimed)
- Member-exp: NPS +5 → 20 referrals × ¥3,000 = **+¥60k/yr**

Three scenarios with mandated multipliers (`templates/07` §3.2 / §3.8):

| Line / 项 | Base (×0.5) / 基准 | Expected (×1.0) / 预期 | Pessimistic (×0) / 悲观 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gross benefit / 总收益 | ¥345k | ¥690k | ¥0 (adoption=0) |
| Cost (capex amort + opex) / 成本 | ¥100k | ¥100k | ¥100k + ¥20k switch |
| Net / 净额 | **+¥245k** | **+¥590k** | **−¥120k** |
| Payback / 回收期 | 0.4 yr | 0.2 yr | n/a (kill) |

Kill criteria (`tools/06` §sensitivity): if adoption <40% at month 3 or benefit <30% of plan at month 6 → stop/pivot. Pessimistic is negative only because adoption=0 — de-risked by a pilot (`templates/08`). Decision: **proceed with a pilot first**, then full buy. Hardware ROI must add 8–15%/yr maintenance to TCO (Industry pitfall #3).

## 5 · Capstone Summaries / 压轴案例摘要

**5.1 · APAC Multi-Market Rollout (Ex.31) / 亚太多市场扩张**
- **EN — Goal:** take a 15-club L3 group from Singapore to Japan+Thailand+Australia over 30 months without a compliance breach. **Action:** one market at a time, ranked by `templates/44`; local SI brokers; global MMS + local payment/messaging adapters; offline-mode + dual ISP after a 9-hr monsoon outage; UTC ingestion to kill DST drift. **Result:** 3 markets live, churn −1.8pp (missed −3pp) but procurement −11% (beat); HK (China) held RED, not killed. **Lesson:** a RED hold is a decision, not a failure; micro-details (name-order, address, holiday calendar) sink launches if unchecked.
- **ZH — 目标：** 30 个月内把新加坡 15 店 L3 集团扩至日/泰/澳而不破合规。 **动作：** 一次一市场、按 `templates/44` 排序；本地 SI 经纪；全球 MMS+本地支付/消息适配；9 小时季风断网后上离线模式+双 ISP；UTC 入库灭 DST 漂移。 **结果：** 三市场在线，流失 −1.8pp（未达 −3pp）但采购 −11%（超标）；中国香港标红暂缓非砍。 **教训：** 红缓是决策非失败；微细节（姓名序、地址式、假期历）不查会沉开业。

**5.2 · AI Transformation of 8000-Member Big-Box (Ex.32) / 8000 会员大型馆 AI 转型**
- **EN — Goal:** lift an L2 big-box to L4 (AI-augmented) in 24 months. **Action:** 6-month data cleanup first (earn L3 before L4); churn model with bias audit; LLM CS behind a kill-switch; CV posture PAUSED at HI-1/HI-5; dynamic pricing KILLED on member-trust signal. **Result:** 5 of 7 AI use cases shipped; retention +2.4pp, CS deflection 38%, energy ~12%; CV/pricing "losses" were risk-avoided wins (no fine, no trust breach). **Lesson:** bias audit and HI-1 are ship gates, not post-mortems; technical win ≠ trust win.
- **ZH — 目标：** 24 个月把 L2 大型馆升至 L4（AI 增强）。 **动作：** 先 6 月数据清洗（先 L3 再 L4）；流失模型带偏见审计；大模型客服带急停；CV 体态于 HI-1/HI-5 暂停；动态定价因会员信任信号被砍。 **结果：** 7 个中 5 个 AI 量产；留存 +2.4pp、客服拦截 38%、能效约 12%；CV/定价「账面亏」实为避险赢（无罚、无破信）。 **教训：** 偏见审计与 HI-1 是上线闸非事后；技术赢≠信任赢。

**5.3 · 30-Unit Franchise Turnaround (Ex.33) / 30 店加盟网络翻身**
- **EN — Goal:** fix a fragmented 30-club franchise (11 MMS, 7 POS, viral complaints) in 18 months. **Action:** diagnose fragmentation; govern via a franchisee council (not decree); phased wave migration with co-invest; NOC + scorecards; rebuild owned growth stack to cap aggregator at 40%. **Result:** unified ID to 93% (28/30), complaints −40%, CAC −22%, owned-funnel 30%→55%; 2 hostile franchisees exited cleanly. **Lesson:** council + waves beat big-bang; a clean exit is a valid outcome; owned funnel is insurance against aggregator term shocks.
- **ZH — 目标：** 18 个月修复碎片化 30 店加盟网（11 套 MMS、7 套 POS、投诉 viral）。 **动作：** 诊断碎片化；经加盟商理事会治（非命令）；分波次迁移+共投；立 NOC 与计分卡；重建自有增长栈将聚合封顶 40%。 **结果：** 统一 ID 达 93%（28/30）、投诉 −40%、CAC −22%、自有漏斗 30%→55%；2 家敌意加盟商干净退出。 **教训：** 理事会+波次胜 big-bang；干净退出是有效结局；自有漏斗是抗聚合变脸的保险。

**5.4 · Zero-Basis Manager First Year (Ex.34) / 零基础经理第一年**
- **EN — Goal:** turn "Mei", an ex-front-desk with zero IT background, into a reliable L1 digital manager in 12 months (2 clubs, ~1,200 members). **Action:** 30-min daily habit (`playbooks/13`); learn 40 terms; run the first outage via runbook; read the SLA/data-export clause on renewal; build a one-page dashboard; scoped pilot + honest year-2 budget ask. **Result:** morning check-in failures 6%→1.2%; renewal saved ~8%; L1 reliable + L2 exposure; won a ¥40–70k year-2 budget. **Lesson:** fit the level (honest ask beat over-reach); a printed checklist is the difference between "I survived" and "I froze."

### 23 · Prepaid-Collapse / 重预付连锁崩塌

- **Root cause (EN):** Prepaid float spent on expansion; no segregated/supervised account; no monthly reconciliation; liability treated as revenue. / **根因（中）：** 预付浮存用于扩张；无隔离监管账户；无月度对账；把负债当收入。
