# Note Due Diligence Checklist — Skill #130

## Description
Systematic due diligence process for buying a real estate note. Covers legal file review, collateral verification, borrower research, title and lien analysis, and state-specific foreclosure risk assessment. Produces a pass/fail checklist and a risk summary before any note purchase.

Use when: evaluating a note prior to purchase, working through a seller's due diligence package, or building a repeatable buying process.

## Trigger Phrases
- "Due diligence on this note"
- "Note due diligence checklist"
- "Should I buy this note?"
- "Review this note file"
- "What do I need to check before buying a note?"
- "Note file review"
- "Verify this note"
- "Pre-purchase note review"

## Inputs Required
- Loan documents (note, deed of trust or mortgage, allonge, assignments)
- Borrower name(s) and property address
- UPB, rate, remaining term, lien position
- Payment history (minimum 24 months preferred)
- Title report or title commitment (or flag as missing)
- BPO or appraisal
- Seller's due diligence package (if provided)
- State the property is in

## What This Skill Produces
1. **Due Diligence Checklist** — 5 categories, each item pass / fail / missing:
   - Legal Documents
   - Collateral / Property
   - Borrower Research
   - Payment History
   - Title and Liens

2. **Risk Summary Card**
   - Overall risk grade (Low / Medium / High / Pass)
   - Key red flags found
   - Items still outstanding
   - Recommended next steps

3. **State Foreclosure Profile** — judicial vs. non-judicial, average timeline, key gotchas for the property's state

4. **Buy / Pass / Negotiate Recommendation** with rationale

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1 — Legal Document Review
Verify the loan file includes:
- [ ] Original promissory note (signed, dated, proper amount)
- [ ] Deed of trust or mortgage (recorded, correct legal description)
- [ ] All allonges (endorsements) creating an unbroken chain of title to the note
- [ ] All recorded assignments to current seller
- [ ] No missing endorsements or gaps in chain of title
- [ ] Lost note affidavit if original note is missing (flag — increases legal risk)

**Red flags:** broken endorsement chain, missing original note without affidavit, incorrect property legal description, recording gaps in assignments.

### Step 2 — Collateral / Property Verification
- [ ] Confirm property address and parcel number via county records
- [ ] Verify property type (SFR, 2-4 unit, condo, commercial)
- [ ] Review BPO/appraisal — is it recent (< 6 months)? Who ordered it?
- [ ] Run basic AVM check (Zillow/Redfin/county assessor) to sanity-check value
- [ ] Confirm occupancy status (owner-occupied, tenant, vacant)
- [ ] Check for active listing (is the borrower trying to sell?)
- [ ] Review exterior photos if available
- [ ] Estimate basis LTV (purchase price ÷ BPO)

**Red flags:** LTV > 85% at basis, vacant property with no insurance, outdated BPO > 12 months, value conflict between BPO and AVM > 20%.

### Step 3 — Borrower Research
- [ ] Verify borrower identity matches loan documents
- [ ] Check for open bankruptcies (PACER search recommended for NPLs)
- [ ] Check for active litigation or judgments against borrower
- [ ] Research borrower's other properties (county records) — are they overwhelmed or strategic?
- [ ] Confirm contact info — can you reach the borrower?
- [ ] Assess borrower motivation: are they trying to stay or walking away?

**Red flags:** Active Chapter 13 (stops foreclosure), multiple judgments, borrower deceased without probate, borrower in active foreclosure litigation.

### Step 4 — Payment History Analysis
- [ ] Review 24-month payment history (minimum)
- [ ] Count days past due and identify pattern (sporadic, declining, no contact)
- [ ] Confirm last payment amount and date
- [ ] Calculate total arrears (missed payments + fees)
- [ ] Assess reinstatement amount
- [ ] Review any prior loss mitigation attempts (loan mods, forbearances)

**Red flags:** No payment in > 12 months with no contact, prior failed loan mod, pattern of 1-2 payments then stopping (strategic default signal).

### Step 5 — Title and Lien Analysis
- [ ] Order title search or review existing title commitment
- [ ] Confirm lien position (1st, 2nd)
- [ ] Check for superior liens (if buying a 2nd)
- [ ] Check for property taxes — are they current? How much in arrears?
- [ ] Check for municipal/HOA liens
- [ ] Check for mechanic's liens or code violation liens
- [ ] Confirm title insurance exists on original loan (if not, flag)
- [ ] Confirm hazard insurance is active

**Red flags:** Tax arrears > 12 months, HOA super-lien in super-lien states, mechanic's liens > $10K, title insurance not available, superior lien amount > BPO equity.

### Step 6 — State Foreclosure Profile
Look up the property state and summarize:
- Judicial vs. non-judicial foreclosure
- Average timeline (months) from default to sale
- Right of redemption period (if any)
- Deficiency judgment availability
- Any state-specific gotchas (e.g., Nevada 2-year redemption, Florida lengthy judicial process)

This directly impacts NPL yield — a 12-month state vs. a 36-month state is a massive difference.

### Step 7 — Compile Risk Summary
Grade each category Low / Medium / High based on findings. Overall grade = worst category. Produce the buy/pass/negotiate recommendation.

## Risk Grading Reference

| Factor | Low Risk | Medium Risk | High Risk |
|--------|----------|-------------|-----------|
| LTV at basis | < 65% | 65–80% | > 80% |
| Lien position | 1st | 2nd (adequate equity) | 2nd (thin equity) |
| Foreclosure timeline | < 9 months | 9–18 months | > 18 months |
| Bankruptcy status | None | Prior/discharged | Active |
| Tax arrears | Current | < 6 months | > 6 months |
| Chain of title | Clean | Minor gap | Major gap / lost note |
