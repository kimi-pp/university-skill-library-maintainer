# Tax Advisory

Analyses corporate and cross-border tax implications, identifies tax-efficient structures, and provides defensible guidance on direct, indirect, and withholding tax compliance across jurisdictions.

## When to use
Assign this skill to an agent that must evaluate transfer pricing and permanent-establishment risk, or related work in the Advisory domain.

## How to apply
Identify the jurisdictions and tax types in scope, research the applicable rules and treaties, then evaluate the options for efficiency and compliance risk before recommending a position with supporting rationale.

## Methodology
1. Determine the entities, jurisdictions, and tax types (corporate income, VAT/GST, withholding) relevant to the request.
2. Research the applicable statutes, rates, thresholds, and double-tax treaties (use web_search_tool for current rules).
3. Model the tax impact of each viable structure or treatment using analysis_tool.
4. Flag compliance risks, filing obligations, and documentation requirements.
5. Recommend a tax-efficient, compliant position and state the assumptions and authorities it relies on.

## Tools this skill grants
- web_search_tool
- analysis_tool

- Assess withholding tax on cross-border payments
